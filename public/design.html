01Colour

All foreground / background pairs verified WCAG 2.1 AA

Calm-clinical palette. Deep teal as the brand and primary action. Warm cream as the page surface — not white, not blush. Status colours kept strictly distinct so they cannot be visually confused: red-flag urgent is the only red on the platform; NICE NG73 prompts are the only deep blue; AI-extracted content is the only purple.

Decision rule. If a new feature needs a status colour, it doesn't get a new hue — it composes from the existing system. Adding a colour means adding a regulatory meaning.

06Motion

Restrained · no decorative animation

Motion is reserved for state transitions and feedback. No celebratory animation on clinical events. Voice capture indicates state change with motion (recording pulse, transcribing dots) but the surrounding UI does not animate.

Anatomy

  • label — left column, ink-muted
  • answer — right column, ink-default; tabular-nums on numeric data
  • flag (optional) — inline, never as a chip
  • sources[] — inline citations after the answer; each is a clickable link

Accessibility

  • Wrapped in <dl> / <dt> / <dd> for semantic association
  • Citations are real links — not buttons; resolvable by keyboard

Regulatory

Every clinical claim cites either source data or a NICE NG73 / ESHRE reference. Banned-term lint runs over answer text — “diagnose”, “definitive”, “you have”, “confirms” fail review. Framings restricted to “suggestive of” / “consistent with”.

05

Red-flag triage callout

The highest-stakes output in the platform. Unambiguous. Single CTA. Never softened. Never auto-dismissable.

Active state — only state

Red flag — seek urgent care now

Heavy bleeding requiring urgent assessment

You have reported soaking through more than one pad per hour for two consecutive hours, with light-headedness. Please contact NHS 111 now, or go to your nearest emergency department.

Anatomy

  • Background · --urgent-100
  • Type · --urgent-800 · 10.1:1 AAA
  • Left border · 3px --urgent-700
  • Primary CTA · btn--urgent (red on white text)

Accessibility

  • role="alert" + aria-live="assertive"
  • Focus moves to the primary CTA on first render
  • No close button; no auto-dismiss timer; no keyboard shortcut to silence
  • Persists across navigations until the trigger condition resolves

Regulatory

The callout's text is rule-engine output, not LLM output. The patient explanation underneath may be LLM-rendered, but the headline and CTA must come from the rule pack. Every render is audit-logged with rule id, rule pack version, and patient response. Tested against fixtures for false-negative and false-positive rates documented in the Risk Management File (ISO 14971).
